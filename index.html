Contact support

Need help? Email darrellbullock@comcast.net with your device model, iOS or Android version, and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

How much does it cost?

Your first full season, playoffs included, is free. The Dynasty Unlock is a one-time purchase that opens every season after it. There are no subscriptions, ads or energy timers.

I bought the Dynasty Unlock on another device, or after reinstalling.

Open Settings, tap Unlock / Restore Purchases, then Restore Purchases, signed in to the same Apple or Google account you bought it with.

Where are my saves?

Saves are stored only on your device. To move a dynasty to another device or keep a backup, use Export Current Dynasty on the Saves screen, then Import From File on the other device. Deleting the app deletes its saves.

Are these real schools or players?

No. Every school, player, coach and league in the game is fictional.

How do I stop sharing usage data?

Go to Settings → Privacy and choose Don’t share. See the privacy policy for details.
