Friday Night Dynasty: Privacy Policy

SupportPrivacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Friday Night Dynasty (“the game”) is made by Evercrest Interactive (“we”). The game is designed to collect as little information as possible. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Your dynasties, save slots and settings are stored only on your device. You can export them to a file yourself. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Data → Delete All Data, removes them.

Anonymous usage analytics (optional)

The game may send anonymous gameplay events, for example “finished first season” or “opened the Dynasty Unlock screen”. They include:

  • the event name and small details such as a game mode or a win-loss record
  • a random install identifier created by the game (not your device’s advertising ID or any hardware identifier)

They never include your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or anything you type. Analytics are not used for advertising and are not sold or shared for tracking. Sharing is on by default; turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Don’t share.

Crash reports

If the game crashes, a diagnostic report (app version, device model, OS version, and the technical error) may be sent to our crash-reporting provider (Sentry). Reports are scrubbed of personal data before leaving your device, including user fields, IP address, device name and file paths.

Purchases

The optional Dynasty Unlock is processed by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt. We never see your name or payment details.

Service providers

We share data only with the providers above (an analytics provider, Sentry and RevenueCat), only for the purposes described, and never sell it. Their handling is covered by their own privacy policies.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can turn off usage analytics in Settings, delete all game data on your device, or delete the app. Because we don’t collect information that identifies you, we can’t look up or link any data to you personally; if you have a question or request, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above.
